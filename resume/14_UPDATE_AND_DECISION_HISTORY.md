## How to update one decision
1. Add a dated source record with exact section/function/row and inspection scope.
2. Add or amend the affected claim, preserve the old conclusion here, and state why it changed.
3. Update only relevant blueprint/matrix/tailoring rows; do not bulk-promote personal skills.
4. Validate source IDs, links, statuses, privacy, counts and the docs-only diff.
5. Reinspect deployment triggers before any future push. A branch name alone is not proof of no deployment.
