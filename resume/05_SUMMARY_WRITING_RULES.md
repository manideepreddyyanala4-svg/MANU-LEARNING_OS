# Professional summary rules
C009, C037–C039, C111–C115 and C148. This is an optional section governed by evidence, not a mandatory Taiwan convention.

## Decision
PERSOL Taiwan recommends a brief summary; Philips discourages objectives. For a current student, use a summary only when it explains relevant fit that is not obvious from the education and project headings. Otherwise omit it. This is INFERENCE under C037, not an employer-wide rule.

## Writing procedure (C038/C043/C072)
Choose one role family. Select one confirmed identity/focus line and, if useful, one directly relevant contribution. Keep the summary short enough to scan; a working target is about two lines, not an enforced word limit. Use factual nouns/methods rather than adjectives like passionate, expert or highly skilled. Do not add every CV and RAG tool to one sentence. Match the JD's vocabulary only when the corresponding personal claim is defensible.

A CV application may foreground C112; a RAG/software application may foreground C113 after ownership verification. Neither current text is ready for unconditional insertion. Mentioning acceptance requires C141 to pass; a public conference proceedings plan is not an acceptance record. Keep permit and detailed schedule in the form/additional line if that communicates fit more clearly (C104/C145).

## Stronger and weaker choices
“AI expert with proven industry impact” has no supported seniority or impact here. A confirmed research focus plus a specific owned task is stronger because each component can be checked (C110–C115/C038). “CV and RAG experience” is still too vague if it conceals AI assistance or the actual task; identify the bounded contribution instead. The blueprint preserves original candidate fragments without assembling a resume.
