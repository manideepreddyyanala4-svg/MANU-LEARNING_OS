Accessed 2026-11-19. C040 governs interpretation: these are legitimate public teaching examples, not verified successful applicants. No private resume was sought; personal contact details are intentionally not reproduced. Text and markup were inspected. Screenshot attempts for Penn State and Da-Yeh failed (cache miss), so visual fidelity/page counts are NOT VERIFIED. The Harvard engineering PDF failed (429) and is excluded from the analyzed count.

## Section and design comparison
Headline/summary is present in some samples and absent in others: it is not an invariant section (C037). Projects, links, named tools, course relevance and attributable outcomes are useful patterns (C038). Publications were not a sufficiently inspected feature of this sample; no claim about their standard placement is derived from these examples. HTML and extracted PDF text show content order, not guaranteed PDF reading order or parser accuracy (C017, C040).

No credible observed example establishes that a photo, icon, two columns, a given font, or a one-page limit causes hiring success. E8's vague metric and E3's generic profile are weak approaches because they fail to explain comparable outcomes or job fit. This judgment is INFERENCE under C038/C040, not a recruiter evaluation of those authors.

## Coverage limits
Only E1/E2/E3/E6/E7/E8/E9 have enough selected content for substantive structural observations; E4/E5 are partial. No verified real Taiwan international M.S. CV/ML or RAG candidate resume with known hiring outcome was located in the searched sources. Official samples and explicitly fictional examples are used as alternatives and labeled accordingly. Do not call the collection nine fully visually audited real-person resumes.
