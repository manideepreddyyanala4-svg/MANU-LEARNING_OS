# Staged Taiwan research: safe consolidated summary
Sources L01–L16; C025–C028/C033–C042/C060–C068. Original research date November 16, 2026. This is a derivative summary, not a new market or scientific verification.

## Market coverage and interpretation
The saved data has 58 role/programme records, 162 Taichung-directory rows and 44 international employer records (C041). “Official JD verified” in the old dataset is a historical audit label: 33 rows had that label, including out-of-scope/programme records. It is not a count of suitable current paid vacancies. The selected n21 primary skill sample and n34 sensitivity sample differ from the final n20 open-only calculation; keep M/P/alternative coding distinct (C025–C027). Counts describe a purposive employer-concentrated sample, not Taiwan-wide demand.

## Corrections retained
- Stage-1 originals were preserved in the private audit archive; later stage-2/3/4 notes change interpretation, not provenance (L01/L15).
- Removed/absent Synology, ITRI U101/U102/F1 and Appier entries should not retain old “apply now” status. Q500 duplicate records must not be counted twice (L03/L15/L16).
- Appier Backend J47 remained in a historical demand table after withdrawal; final open-only sensitivity excludes it (C025).
- Earlier roadmaps/allocations are planning judgments and tasks. Topic counts and updated prerequisite/hour distinctions do not demonstrate completed personal skills (C035).
- Legal, permit, graduation, onsite/commute and working-language checks were unresolved personally. A programme-level stipend is not an individual part-time wage (L01/L11/L16).
- Dorsal reports distinguish historical target-trained and source-only protocols; no current final metric was independently validated. Reproduction is not personal architecture invention (C028).
- EvidenceOps was AI-assisted. Saved offline tests are bounded software evidence, not personal mastery, production performance or deployment (C029–C033).

## Retained source fields
The three CSV extracts preserve useful public role/company information without the full source-snapshot corpus. Their dates and historical status values are retained. Fresh Appier-duration and other November 19 checks are recorded in the main knowledge-base files, not silently written back into old snapshots (C022/C073).
